Scenario selection (written before screening)
Candidates: 4 Chennai corridors, ~4 km long, plus the current area.
Screening: fixed timing only, 10 screening seeds (101-110), at 3x and 5x traffic.
Choose the candidate with the highest share of the ambulance's trip stopped at red signals at 5x,
among candidates where the route crosses at least 5 signals and all 10 runs finish.
All candidates are reported, including the rejected ones.
Measure: seconds the ambulance is stopped (below 1 m/s) within 250 m of a red signal, as a share of trip time.
Scale per map: the highest tested traffic scale at which all 10 fixed-timing screening runs finish. Maps that gridlock at the lowest tested scale are rejected.
